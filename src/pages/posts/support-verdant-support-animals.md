# How Verdant Champions Animal Welfare

At Verdant, we do not only serve plant-based food; we also foresee a world that deals humanely with animals. If you eat at Verdant, you will become an active contributor to a movement that puts animal well-being, sustainability, and an ethical way of life at the forefront.

## Farm Partnerships for Ethical Impact
We work with over **80 local farms** that practice cruelty-free and ethical farming practices, which do not cause harm to animals during production. These farms prioritize biodiversity and regenerative agriculture, which yields advantages to rejuvenate natural systems and protect animal habitats.

## Every Meal Saves Lives
Every plant-based meal served at Verdant contributes to reducing factory farming demand, which is responsible for **over 70 billion land animals** being slaughtered annually. By replacing meat plates with tasty vegan meals, we have redirected an estimated **50,000 pounds** of yearly meat consumption. This helps reduce animal suffering and environmental degradation.

## Direct Support to Animal Sanctuaries
A portion of our profits—**10% of overall revenue**—go directly to animal rescue groups and sanctuaries. Our donation has helped rehab **hundreds of rescued farm animals** to provide them with refuge, medical care, and a safe environment to thrive.

## Eco-Friendly Initiatives Protecting Wildlife
Verdant's sustainability doesn't stop at food. Our **zero-waste packaging, plastic-free dining, and tree-planting initiatives** ensure our impact is minimal. In association with organizations like Ecosia, our operations have planted over **5,000 trees**, restoring habitats for thousands of species of wildlife.

## Join the Movement
When you dine at Verdant, you're joining the movement. You're stating food doesn't have to be just good or only good for the animals. You're helping animals by helping out Verdant, and together, we can create a better world for all.
